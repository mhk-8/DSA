# Weekly Plan

## Linked Lists — 4 weeks (~1.5 hrs/day)

**Week 1 — Foundation**
Re-read Part 0 of the guide until pointers feel obvious. Complete
`03_Linked_Lists/practice/practice-01-basics.cpp` (all 10 TODOs) without
looking at the solutions. Then LeetCode 707, 876, 206, 203, 83.

**Week 2 — The three techniques**
Finish Tier 1. Write the dummy-node, fast/slow, and reversal templates from
memory three days in a row. Start Tier 2: 19, 2, 142, 92, 82.

**Week 3 — Composite problems**
Tier 2 remainder: 24, 61, 86, 328, 143, 148, 138. Name the sub-patterns aloud
as you decompose each one. Then write LRU Cache (146) from scratch, twice, on
different days.

**Week 4 — Hardening**
Tier 3. Then re-solve ten Tier 2 problems on a timer, 20 minutes each, on
paper, no compiler. This is the closest thing to the real interview.
